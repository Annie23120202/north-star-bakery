About North Star Bakery

Our Neighborhood Story

North Star Bakery is a sample neighborhood bakery website centered on sharing handmade food and welcoming the community. Our goal is to make each visit feel personal, from morning bread to a cake for a special event.

Our Approach to Ingredients

We value fresh ingredients, careful preparation, and clear communication about what goes into each order. Ask our team about ingredients and allergies before requesting a pre-order.

Meet the People Behind the Counter

Our bakers focus on preparing bread and sweets; our front-counter staff help customers choose treats and discuss special orders. These are sample staff roles for the student project, not biographies of actual employees.
